Week 5 - Design Section

17 March, 2025/p> Primary Goal: My primary goal for my first website is to showcase all my creative work in an aesthetically pleasing manner which is clean and efficient for my users. I also want the website to reflect my personality and this is seen through the colour and design choices> A minimalistic website is great way of mirroring the kind of Digital Art girlie I am lol.

Content Mapping

Home Page: Introduction, links to other pages or sections About Page: Author name, background, qualifications and skills Blogs Page: Links to all the weekly blogs Essays Page: Links to all essays Design Page: Gallery of my design work for the website, wireframes and styleguides.

Navigation Structure: Header Navigation: Home, About, Blogs, Design, Essays, Portfolio Footer: Contact info, social media links

Interface Elements

Navigation bar: Will be at the top right of each page

: For call to action eg: back buttons

Typography Hierarchy:

1. Headings for sections

2. Subheadings

3. Body for content

Visual Aesthetic and Design Process

1. Neutral Palette: Shades of beige, white, grey and a little touch of black specifically for the text across the pages.

2. Typography: Texts and styles that are clean and readable

Fonts:

Headings Font: Merriweather for readability

Body Font: Montserrat

Colours

A neutral palette was chosen for simplicity

Text Colour: Black

Background: Colour: White

Primary Colour: Light Grey

Accent Colour: Beige/Dark Grey

Buttons:

Button Shape: Semi-Rounded corners for a minimalistic and soft look

Button Colour: Buttons on the nav bar will be black with white text, while previous and next buttons will be grey with black text on blog pages.

Hierarchy:

H1: Headings

H2&H3: Subheadings and descriptions

Page Layout:

Alignment: Left

Links: Should be in bold for a clear distinction
